# Roadmap

## MVP (class prototype)

Authentication, roles, vehicles, mileage, workshops, links, maintenance, operating expenses, history, basic reports, and in-app alerts.

## Post-MVP

- Email and WhatsApp delivery hardening.
- Receipt attachments and OCR.
- Appointment scheduling and workshop messaging.
- Fleet/company accounts and multiple workshop branches.
- Automated odometer capture and advanced cost analytics.

Do not implement post-MVP features until the MVP workflow is polished and demonstrable.
