# Project Overview

## Product

AutoAlert centralizes vehicle records, maintenance, repairs, operating expenses, workshops, history, reports, and reminders. It helps owners avoid missed services and retain a trustworthy mechanical history.

## Problem

Drivers and small fleets often track oil changes, filters, tires, insurance, registration, and repairs in scattered notes or not at all. This leads to missed deadlines, unplanned breakdowns, higher costs, and incomplete records.

## Target users

- Private vehicle owners with one or more vehicles.
- Mechanics who manage one or more workshops.
- Future market: transport businesses, delivery companies, taxi drivers, and small fleets.

## Core value

The two primary features are: (1) a filterable monthly vehicle history and (2) date-, mileage-, and expiry-based notifications.

## Product summary

Owners register vehicles, mileage, services, repairs, fuel, insurance, and registration. Mechanics create public workshops and register services through their own workshop. Vehicle–workshop links allow selected owner and mechanic records to be shared safely.
